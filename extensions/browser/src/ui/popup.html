Set up NewPGP in about 5 minutes

Step 1 of 3

1. Install the helper

A small local program does the locking. It keeps your key on your computer. You set it up once — there is nothing to keep running.

Windows: native-host/install.ps1 Mac or Linux: native-host/install.sh

Need the exact steps? Open the setup notes.

2. Make your key

Enter your name and the email you send from. Your key stays on your computer.

This is the name people see beside your messages. Use the address you usually send mail from.

3. Write normally

Write your email as usual. We keep your words readable and add an encrypted copy underneath, so everyone can still read it.

Want to change that? Use the “Send PGP only” switch below.

Done — open Gmail Open Outlook

How it works
  1. You write a normal email in Gmail or Outlook.
  2. NewPGP asks the local NewPGP helper to encrypt a copy for your contacts' keys.
  3. We put the encrypted block at the bottom of your message, unless you chose PGP only.
  4. Incoming encrypted blocks get a “Decrypt with NewPGP” button.

Your message text is never stored by the extension. Webmail providers may still autosave drafts before sending — see the options page for details.
